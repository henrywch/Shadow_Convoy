Conclusion · one city, three lenses tiered consensus registry
No single primitive sees this city. The three methods triangulate — their agreement is the high-confidence signal, their gaps are intrinsic blindnesses, not bugs.
TIER 1
GOLD triple-witnessed. Present in FP-Growth output AND covered by a MaxGrowth platoon AND inside a confirmed embedding cluster. Highest precision — the operational core.
TIER 2
SILVER double-witnessed. Two-of-three agreement: large embedding fleets (MaxGrowth-fragmented but real) and small FP-Growth convoys (embedding-missed but real).
TIER 3
CAND. single-method. Investigative leads only; manual triage & parameter-sensitivity studies.
The shadow convoy was never one shadow. It was the union of three shadows cast by three different lights — and the city only shows what's at the intersection.
