FP-Growth cells · itemset mining
support(I) = #cells containing I  ≥  min_count
Plate-sets co-occurring in many (camera, time-bucket) cells. Undirected, direct evidence.
Method detail
  • Cell = one camera × one ε-window. Itemset = the plates that landed in it.
  • Spark MLlib FP-Growth at min_count=50 over dual-offset bucketisation.
  • Strength: high-precision small permanent convoys. Weakness: C(N, k) blowup on large fleets.
  • Two dual-offset grids (offset 0 and ε/2) absorb plates whose visits straddle a bucket boundary.
Pipeline Spark MLlib
ε=300s min_count=50 cap=2000
Engineering notes
  • density_cap=2000: caps freak buckets (one camera once had 23,595 plates — stuck reader); largest legitimate bucket is 1,718.
  • -Xss=16m on executor JVM — FP-tree path serialisation needs > default 1 MB stack.
  • No df.persist(): a single scan is faster than caching 13 GiB cluster-wide.
  • Output schema: (plates, k, count).

MaxGrowth routes · VLDB '24
⟨O, P⟩ · ∀p∈O: p visits every c∈P in order
Directed routes co-traversed by a group of plates. The route is the proof.
Method detail
  • ε-co-arrival at each route stop, max d intermediate cameras skipped per member, |O|≥m and |P|≥k.
  • Maximal: emit only the longest /widest pattern — not dominated by any ⟨O', P'⟩ with O⊆O' and P a d-subseq of P'.
  • Reference: Bei et al., "Mining Platoon Patterns from Traffic Videos", VLDB 2024.
  • The directed-route requirement makes MaxGrowth far harder to fake than co-occurrence buckets.
Pipeline Spark + driver recursion
m=2 k=3 d=1 ε=300s min_obs=200 max_cluster=50
Engineering notes
  • Local maximality pruning inside grow_from_root — skip emission when any extension preserves full membership. ~10-100× less raw output.
  • Inverted-index dominance filter — candidates restricted to patterns containing every member of B, seeded on B's rarest plate. Turns 19 h naive scan into seconds.
  • Broadcast envelope: trajectories + position index + clusters-by-camera, < 1 GiB after prefilter.
  • Failure modes hit + fixed: master-node OOM (memory rebalance), cluster-count blowup (max_cluster cap), Java stack overflow in Kryo (`-Xss=16m`).

Embedding t2vec + HDBSCAN
h = GRUenc( corrupt(x) )  ·  eplate = hfinal ∈ ℝ256
Route-shape vectors. Plates with similar month-long routes cluster; co-occurrence confirms.
Method detail
  • Denoising seq-to-seq AE (t2vec, Li et al. ICDE 2018) repurposed for ANPR camera trajectories.
  • Encoder corrupts input (30 % token drop), decoder reconstructs the clean route — buys occlusion / OCR robustness.
  • Confirmation step (rare in trajectory clustering) re-reads the raw CSV and keeps only clusters whose members actually co-occur in ε-windows.
  • Without confirmation, two commuters driving the same road every morning would look identical to a real convoy.
Pipeline Spark → PyTorch → cuML
hidden=256 epochs=10 buckets=32 tbptt=256 drop=0.3 min_cooccur=3
Engineering notes
  • bf16 autocast — same exponent range as fp32, half the memory, no overflow.
  • Bucket batching (32 buckets, length-grouped) — one long bus trajectory no longer pads every batch to 3,202 tokens.
  • TBPTT (chunk = 256) — decoder gradient capped, no exploding-NaN spirals.
  • Failure modes hit + fixed: NCCL NVLS multicast bug on 4× GPUs (env workaround), cache build redundancy (rank-0 pickle), CUDA driver/runtime version mismatch.
  • Loss trajectory: 2.84 → 2.18 over 10 epochs, monotonic, no NaN, 2 batches rescued by nan_to_num_.

Conclusion · one city, three lenses tiered consensus
No single primitive sees this city. The three methods triangulate — agreement is signal; gaps are intrinsic blindness, not bug.
TIER 1GOLD Triple-witnessed. FP-Growth ∩ MaxGrowth ∩ Embedding-confirmed. Operational core.
TIER 2SILVER Double-witnessed. Large embedding fleets (MaxGrowth-fragmented) or small FP-Growth convoys (embedding-missed).
TIER 3CAND. Single-method. Investigative leads only.
